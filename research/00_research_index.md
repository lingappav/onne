## The Research Spine (How the Six Connect)

```
DANDANITI (the standard)
    ↓
The state is legitimate only when its coercion is proportionate and just.
    ↓
ONOE + ARTICLE 356 (the mechanism)
    ↓
The Centre imposes President's Rule on Karnataka — using a constitutional provision
for administrative convenience. This is tikshna danda: excess punishment of a state
for the offence of having its own democratic timetable.
    ↓
MEDIA TRIALS (the cover)
    ↓
The real mechanism of imposition is hidden behind a spectacle of corruption
allegations against the state government. While the camera covers the scandal,
the administrative machinery of dismissal operates.
    ↓
CRIMES WITHOUT TECHNOLOGY (the pattern)
    ↓
This is not exceptional. Every major harm done to Indians by their own state
followed this pattern: lawful process, bureaucratic chain, no single actor
bearing full responsibility.
    ↓
DHARMIC REVENGE (the protagonist's response)
    ↓
The protagonist — watching this happen to the government he served — asks
the Mahabharata's question: when the normal order of justice fails,
what is permitted?
    ↓
KARNATAKA (the stakes)
    ↓
What is being destroyed is not just a government. It is a civic tradition
that took 900 years to build — from Basavanna's Anubhava Mantapa to
Visvesvaraya's engineering to the Mysuru compact to Bangalore's pluralism.
```
