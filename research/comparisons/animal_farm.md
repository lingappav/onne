# Animal Farm — Comparison to The President's Rule

**Author:** George Orwell  |  **Year:** 1945

## Reference Book Notes

The cleanest example of a short political novel that lands a sustained ideological argument through character. PRS should study how Orwell makes policy itself feel dramatic.

## Shared Themes (3)

- language-as-control
- corruption-of-power
- betrayal-of-revolution

## Structural Parallels

- Both use a 3-act structure
- PRS Master Narrative is 33.1% the length of Animal Farm

## Claimed Comparable Aspects (from catalog)

- how-power-corrupts-cleanly
- individual-as-vessel-of-system
- language-distortion-as-weapon

## Differentiators

- reference has 'political-allegory', not central to PRS
