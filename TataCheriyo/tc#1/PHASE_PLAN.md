# tc#1 — Phase 1: PLANT THE SEED

> "First, you must make them curious. They don't have to love the film. They have to remember the banner."

**Release Day:** Day 45
**Phase Window:** Day 1 – Day 60 (15-day theatrical tail included)
**Audience Goal:** Curiosity. The city should be asking, *"Who made this?"*

---

## 1. Phase Mission

This is the **smallest, sharpest, riskiest** film of the three. Its job is not to make money. Its job is to make TataCheriyo a name worth saying out loud at a chai stall on Brigade Road.

**Brand objective:** plant the seed.
**Commercial objective:** recover 60–70% of cost theatrically; balance from OTT.
**Cultural objective:** earn one critic review that uses the phrase *"a new voice."*

---

## 2. Format

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| **Runtime** | 70–80 minutes |
| **Genre** | Tight contained drama OR slow-burn thriller (something that doesn't need scale to land) |
| **Language** | Kannada, with English subtitles for OTT |
| **Locations** | 3–4 total. Mostly Bangalore interiors. |
| **Cast size** | 4–6 principal speaking roles |
| **VFX** | Minimal / none |
| **Music** | One score, sparse. Establishes the TataCheriyo sonic motif. |
| **Budget tier** | ~₹66 L total |

**Concept sweet spot:** something a single brilliant location and a single brilliant actor can carry. Think *Lunchbox* economics with *Andhadhun* tension.

### Release (Day 45)

- **Opening:** 25 screens in Bangalore
- **Configuration:** Anchor multiplex (PVR/Cinepolis) + 3 single-screens + 18 multiplex satellites
- **Showtimes:** Lock 6:30 PM and 9:30 PM for opening week
- **First weekend target:** 60% occupancy across the 25 screens

### Theatrical tail (Days 46–60)

- Week 2: drop to 15 screens, evening shows only
- Week 3: drop to 5–8 screens, single evening show
- Day 60: theatrical close
- OTT release Day 65 (Aha / Sun NXT) — synced with tc#2 first-look

## 5. Casting Strategy

- **No star.** A "name" inflates budget without proportionally lifting Phase 1's brand task.
- **One revelation.** Cast an actor the city hasn't seen lead before. The discovery becomes part of the story of the film.
- **Theatre actors over film actors.** Ranga Shankara, Samudaya, Suchitra alumni — they bring discipline and screen freshness.
- **One familiar face in a supporting role.** Anchors the film for older audiences.

---

## 8. Risks Specific to Phase 1

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| First-film jitters extend the shoot | Hard 10-day cap. Producer holds the line. |
| The "new voice" review never comes | Pre-cultivate 3 critics Day 1–20 with banner-vision conversations, not film pitches |
| Banner is invisible because the film is loud | The TataCheriyo logo is the **first frame and the last frame** of the film |
| Friday-release competition from a big Kannada film | Check release calendar Day 1 and choose a clean Friday. Don't fight a Yash / Shivanna film. |
| Single-screen advance bookings weak | Partner with student film clubs (Christ, MCC, NLSIU) for opening-week block bookings |

---

## 9. Handoff to Phase 2

On Day 45 (release day), the banner does **three** things to set up tc#2:
1. Drops the **tc#2 first-look poster** in the end-credits and immediately on social
2. Hands the editor's chair to tc#2's edit cycle
3. Books the same anchor multiplex slot for Day 80

The audience must leave the theatre having seen tc#1 **and** knowing that tc#2 is already coming.
