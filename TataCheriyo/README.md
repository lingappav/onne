# TataCheriyo — 120-Day Three-Film Slate

**Banner:** TataCheriyo
**Mission:** Three theatrical-release films in Bangalore inside 100–120 days, executed as **three incremental phases of planting a dream in the social mind.**

> Each film is not a standalone product. It is **one act of a single audience-building campaign.** Phase 1 plants the seed, Phase 2 deepens the world, Phase 3 lands the dream.

---

## 1. Strategic Intent

This is not three random films released in sequence. It is **one campaign in three movements**, designed so that the audience built by each film carries into the next.

| Phase | Folder | Movement | Audience Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | `tc#1` | **PLANT** — the seed | Curiosity. "Who is TataCheriyo?" |
| **Phase 2** | `tc#2` | **DEEPEN** — the world | Conviction. "TataCheriyo is making the films I wasn't getting elsewhere." |
| **Phase 3** | `tc#3` | **LAND** — the dream | Identification. "I am part of TataCheriyo's audience." |

By Phase 3, the brand has done the marketing the film no longer has to pay for.

---

## 2. Format & Scale Decisions (the only way 3 films in 120 days is possible)

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Length** | 70–95 min features (no 150-min epics) | Shorter shoot, faster post, more shows per screen per day |
| **Language** | Kannada primary, with strategic English / Hindi titles | Bangalore primary, OTT-ready secondary |
| **Genre rotation** | Different genre per phase, **same thematic spine** | Avoid audience fatigue, build range without diluting brand |
| **Crew** | One core technical crew across all three films | DOP, editor, sound, colourist are continuous — only cast & locations rotate |
| **Production model** | **Staggered parallel** — pre-prod, shoot, post, release all overlap across the three films | The only way the math works inside 120 days |
| **Distribution** | Hybrid — theatrical in Bangalore + immediate OTT/YouTube tail | Theatrical = brand, OTT = recoup |
| **Theatres** | Independent + PVR/INOX/Cinepolis multiplex satellites in Bangalore — 25–60 screen openings | Realistic for an indie banner |

## 4. Core Continuous Crew (the engine room)

Hire **once**, deploy across all three:

- **Producer / Line Producer** — owns the calendar across all 3
- **Director of Photography** — same visual signature builds brand
- **Editor** — picks up the next film while previous is in colour
- **Sound Designer** — same continuous studio
- **Colourist** — single DI suite, blocked for 120 days
- **Music Director** — composes a **TataCheriyo sonic motif** that recurs (one bar) across all three films
- **Production Designer** — rotating locations, consistent texture
- **Casting Director** — pipeline for all 3 in parallel
- **Marketing & Social Lead** — manages the brand arc, not the film arc
- **Theatrical Liaison (Bangalore)** — single point of contact for PVR/INOX/Cinepolis/single-screens

**Rotating per film:** director (optional — one director can do all three if able), writers, cast, location managers.

---

## 5. The Single Thematic Spine

Every TataCheriyo film must answer **one** unifying question — chosen now and never revealed in marketing, but visible to anyone who watches all three.

Choose one from:
- *What does Bangalore owe the people who built it?*
- *What survives in a city when its memory is overwritten?*
- *Where is the line between dreaming and delusion?*
- *Whose city is this?*

Pick the question in Day 1. Every script, every poster, every poster colour, every closing shot gestures toward it. **This is the dream being planted.**

## 6. The Bangalore Theatrical Strategy

| Lever | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Opening screens** | tc#1: 25 screens. tc#2: 40. tc#3: 60+. Growth is the story. |
| **Anchor multiplex** | One PVR or Cinepolis as the "home theatre" — all three films open there, same screen, same Friday slot. Becomes a ritual. |
| **Single-screen** | Partner with 3–4 single screens (Sagar, Navrang, Veeresh, Santosh, etc.) for cultural credibility |
| **Showtimes** | Lock 6:30 PM and 9:30 PM daily for opening week — the prime-time slots that signal seriousness |
| **Ticket pricing** | Standard. Don't discount the brand into a charity. |
| **Pre-release events** | Trailer launch at Town Hall / Ranga Shankara / Suchitra. Cultural framing, not Bollywood-style hype |
| **Press** | Deccan Herald + Vijay Karnataka + The Hindu (Bangalore) — book 3 anchor interviews per film |
| **Social** | Single TataCheriyo handle; films are children of it, not separate brands |
| **Festival overlay** | Submit each film to BIFFes (Bangalore International Film Festival) and IFFI Goa — even if it releases first, festival selection lifts brand |

## 8. Risk Register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Shoot overrun on tc#1 cascades into tc#2** | Hard 10-day cap on tc#1. Director and producer sign a written discipline pact. |
| **Post bottleneck** | Single DI suite booked for full 120 days. Editor starts tc#2 while tc#1 is in colour. |
| **One film flops, kills momentum for next** | Brand-first marketing — TataCheriyo is the protagonist, not any single film |
| **Star unavailability** | Cast emerging actors with strong screen presence. No film depends on a "name." |
| **Screen availability** | Lock the anchor multiplex by Day 1 with a slate deal, not film-by-film |
| **Cash flow** | Stagger funding tranches against milestone completions, not against calendar dates |
| **Quality drop in Phase 3 from fatigue** | Mandatory 5-day rest gap between tc#2 wrap and tc#3 shoot for core crew |
| **Bangalore monsoon (June–Sept) disrupts outdoor shoots** | Plan outdoor blocks for Day 1–60 if starting pre-monsoon; otherwise build interior-heavy scripts |

## 9. Daily Cadence (operating rhythm)

- **Morning standup (9 AM, 15 min)** — producer + line producer + the active-film director. What ships today?
- **Wednesday slate review (1 hr)** — all three phase leads. Where is each film? What's the next bottleneck?
- **Friday weekly metric** — single page: days-to-release, screens-locked, edit-progress %, P&A spend, social reach, advance bookings.
- **Phase handoff ceremony** — short, intentional. When one film moves from shoot to post, the crew gathers, marks it, moves on the same week.
