# tc#2 — Phase 2: DEEPEN THE WORLD

> "Now they know the banner. They are curious but not yet convinced. Phase 2 is where curiosity becomes conviction."

**Release Day:** Day 80
**Phase Window:** Day 11 (parallel pre-prod begins) – Day 95 (theatrical tail)
**Audience Goal:** Conviction. The city should be saying, *"TataCheriyo is making the films I wasn't getting elsewhere."*

---

## 1. Phase Mission

If Phase 1 introduced a voice, Phase 2 proves it wasn't an accident. It must be **clearly recognisable** as a TataCheriyo film while being **clearly different** from tc#1. Same DNA, different organ.

**Brand objective:** deepen the world. Establish range without losing identity.
**Commercial objective:** break even theatrically. OTT profit on top.
**Cultural objective:** the phrase "*the TataCheriyo film*" enters common usage in Bangalore film conversation.

---

## 2. Format

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| **Runtime** | 85–95 minutes |
| **Genre** | Different from tc#1 but in the same thematic universe (if tc#1 was a quiet drama → tc#2 is an ensemble dark comedy, OR a relationship piece, OR a stylised noir) |
| **Language** | Kannada, with strategic Hindi/English code-switching for OTT crossover |
| **Locations** | 6–8 total. Mix of Bangalore exteriors + interiors. One signature location. |
| **Cast size** | 6–10 principal speaking roles. Ensemble feel. |
| **VFX** | Minimal — one set-piece moment that justifies seeing it on a big screen |
| **Music** | Score + 2 songs (carrying the TataCheriyo sonic motif forward, evolved) |
| **Budget tier** | ~₹1.02 Cr total |

**Concept sweet spot:** the film that proves the banner can do more than the first film. A wider canvas, a denser ensemble, a slightly bigger swing — but **not** the climax. Save the climax for tc#3.

## 5. Casting Strategy

- **Bring back one face from tc#1** in a different role. This is the *"is that the same actor?"* easter egg that signals universe-building.
- **One mid-tier name** — an actor with a Sandalwood track record but not a "star" — to widen reach.
- **Ensemble principle:** no single lead. Three or four people share the weight.
- Continue the theatre-actor pipeline established in tc#1.

## 7. The Universe Hooks (this is the new lever in Phase 2)

Phase 2 introduces **shared-world signals** that reward tc#1 viewers and confuse tc#2 newcomers in a productive way:

- One character mentions an event from tc#1 by name (background line, not exposition)
- One location appears in both films (a tea stall, a junction, a building)
- The TataCheriyo sonic motif appears at the same narrative beat type (e.g., every act-2 turn)
- A visual: a poster or graffiti in the background of tc#2 references tc#1's title or imagery

By Phase 3, this universe will pay off enormously. Phase 2's job is to plant the hooks.

## 9. Risks Specific to Phase 2

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Sophomore slump — tc#2 is "just another film" | Plant the universe hooks early in writing. They are the differentiator. |
| Crew fatigue from back-to-back production | Mandatory 5-day gap between tc#1 shoot wrap and tc#2 shoot start. One rest day in tc#2 shoot. |
| tc#1 underperformed → tc#2 has weaker tailwind | The release rhythm is the brand. Maintain it. Don't postpone. |
| Bigger cast = bigger ego management | Casting director vets attitude during casting, not just talent. One ego in an ensemble breaks the math. |
| Two-song requirement bloats the music budget | Lock music director on a slate deal — three films, not per-song |
| Trailer reveals too much (over-correction from tc#1's mystery posture) | Director, editor, and marketing lead must sign off the trailer cut together |

## 10. Handoff to Phase 3

On Day 80 (release day), the banner does **four** things to set up tc#3:
1. Drops the **tc#3 trailer** in end-credits (not a teaser — a trailer; the audience has earned it)
2. Hands the editor's chair to tc#3
3. Books **double** the screens for tc#3 with the anchor multiplex
4. Announces the **slate-wrap event** for Day 120 — turns the brand's first slate into an *event*

The Phase 2 audience must leave the theatre understanding that **tc#3 is the one they came for all along** — even though they didn't know it on Day 1.
