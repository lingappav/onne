# Research Topics — *The President's Rule*

Six research strands that the novel weaves into its political and philosophical fabric.
Each strand has its own working file with arguments, sources, and reading list — and each is the credibility hook for a different category of patron.

| # | Topic | Working file | Patron alignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Chanakya's Dandaneeti — punishment for peace** | [01_dandaneeti.md](01_dandaneeti.md) | Cultural philanthropists, Indic-thought patrons |
| 2 | **Media trials in India — how the spectacle hides the truth** | [02_media_trials.md](02_media_trials.md) | Press-freedom advocates, civic-discourse patrons |
| 3 | **Dharma-justified revenge — the ethics of retribution** | [03_dharmic_revenge.md](03_dharmic_revenge.md) | Literary patrons, scholars of Indian thought |
| 4 | **Why Karnataka — what binds people to this state** | [04_karnataka_love.md](04_karnataka_love.md) | Bangalore-rooted patrons, Karnataka-pride sponsors |
| 5 | **One Nation, One Election — federal balance under stress** | [05_one_nation_one_election.md](05_one_nation_one_election.md) | Governance reformers, policy-engaged patrons |
| 6 | **The greatest crimes done without technology** | [06_crimes_without_technology.md](06_crimes_without_technology.md) | Civic-minded patrons, historical-political readers |

The novel does not write essays about these. It dramatises them through character. The essays here are the *research substrate* — what gets written so the fiction can stand on real ground.

Each topic also exists as a **standalone Substack post** in the writing year, which doubles as a patron-funnel.
