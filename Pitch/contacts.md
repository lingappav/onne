## STREAM C — Grants, Advances, Big Tickets

### Indian Writing Grants

| Org | Grant | Amount | Cycle | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **India Foundation for the Arts (IFA)** | Arts Practice grant | Up to ₹5L (~$6K) | Rolling | The big one. Strong fit for a political novel. Apply Day 20. |
| **Sangam House** | Residency | In-kind ($2K-equivalent) | Annual | Frees you from rent/food during writing block; converts to fundable runway |
| **Toto Funds the Arts** | Emerging-writer grant | ₹1–2L | Annual | Age cap; check eligibility |
| **Charles Wallace India Trust** | UK residency for Indian writers | ~£2,500 stipend | Annual deadline ~ Oct/Nov | Different timeline; long shot for the 90-day window |
| **South Asia Speaks fellowship** | Mentorship + small stipend | ~$500–$1,000 | Annual | Mentorship is the real value |

**For each:** read past awardees, find one you know or can connect to, ask for 15 minutes of advice before applying. Always apply, but warm-route first.

## Outreach Sequence Recommendation (use with [outreach_templates.md](outreach_templates.md))

**Week 1:** Tier-1 inner circle (8 people, ₹40K asks) — in person or phone calls only
**Week 2:** Sangam / Toto / IFA grant applications submitted
**Week 3–4:** Tier-2 mid circle (15 people, ₹10K asks) — personalised emails / WhatsApp
**Week 5:** Substack public push + cross-promotion swaps
**Week 6–7:** Tier-3 outer circle (30 people, ₹5K asks)
**Week 8:** Publisher / agent submissions (3 of each)
**Week 9:** Podcast pitches (4–6)
**Week 10:** Print feature pitches (3–5)
**Week 11–12:** Final follow-ups + in-person Bangalore event

## Two Lists to Build Right Now (Day 1 task)

1. **The Tier-1 Eight.** Write 8 names of people you are 80% sure will say yes to a ₹40K ask. If you can't get to 8 — that's the bottleneck, not the budget.
2. **The Believer Ten.** Write 10 people who would champion your work *publicly* (post, retweet, recommend). These cost nothing but they unlock everything else.

Until those 18 names are written, the rest of this plan is theoretical.
