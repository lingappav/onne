## Template 2 — One-Line Pitch (rehearse it cold)

> *"I'm writing a political novel about constitutional collapse in India, set in Karnataka — Sunday Cinemas optioned the film rights last February — and I'm raising nine thousand dollars to write it full-time for ninety days. Will you support a chapter?"*

**Why it works:**
- *Subject* — political novel, constitutional collapse, Karnataka
- *Stakes* — implied national relevance
- *Credibility* — Sunday Cinemas option (the thing 95% of writers don't have)
- *Specific ask* — $9K / 90 days
- *Micro-commitment* — "a chapter" (₹10K) is the foot in the door

Workshop variations:
- For grants: replace "Will you support a chapter?" with "We're applying for the [Grant Name] cycle."
- For publishers: replace with "Looking for a home — could we talk?"
- For podcasters: replace with "Would your audience care?"

## Template 3 — Short Synopsis (1 page)

```
THE PRESIDENT'S RULE  —  Synopsis  (1 page)

GENRE     Literary political fiction
LENGTH    Approx. 90,000 words
SETTING   Bangalore, Delhi, Belagavi  •  2024–2026
COMPS     [Comp 1] meets [Comp 2]; in the tradition of
          [Comp 3] but with the urgency of [Comp 4].

THE STORY

[Paragraph 1 — set up the world. Bangalore in 2024.
The protagonist's everyday life. Hint of the larger
political weather.]

[Paragraph 2 — the inciting incident. The Governor
invokes President's Rule. The protagonist is pulled
in by [specific event].]

[Paragraph 3 — rising action. The choice that sharpens.
The cost of each side. The character pivot point.]

[Paragraph 4 — climax. The act that cannot be taken
back. The book's central image.]

[Paragraph 5 — the ending. Not resolution — recognition.
What the novel finally says about the question it asked.]

WHY THIS BOOK NOW

[Two sentences on the political moment. Two sentences on
what fiction adds that journalism cannot.]
```

Standard publisher/agent format. Three-act structure with named characters. Every major beat. End reveals the ending — agents and editors *need* to know the ending.

Use the [Master Narrative](../manuscripts/The_Presidents_Rule_Master_Narrative.md) as the source. The long synopsis is the beat-level compression of it.

## Template 5 — Sample Chapter (Chapter 1)

Already in your manuscript pipeline. **Polish to publication quality.** Have it read by 2 trusted readers before sending out. This is the single most important artefact — most agents and publishers will read past page 1 only if page 1 earns it.

**Checklist for Chapter 1:**
- [ ] Opening line that locates voice and stakes
- [ ] Within first 3 pages: the world, the protagonist, the tension
- [ ] No exposition dumps
- [ ] Ends on a hook that demands page-turning

## Template 9 — Comparable Titles ("Comps")

Three to five books that:
- Share themes with your book (political, constitutional, India, federalism)
- Are not 30 years old (recent enough to signal market)
- Sold well or won notable recognition

Use this format on your one-pager and in pitches:

> *"In the tradition of **[BOOK A]** for its political density, **[BOOK B]** for its Karnataka setting, and **[BOOK C]** for its readability — but with the constitutional urgency of **[BOOK D]**."*

Candidate comps to evaluate (depending on tone):
- *A Suitable Boy* — Vikram Seth (Indian political-social fiction, scale)
- *The White Tiger* — Aravind Adiga (Bangalore, sharp, commercial)
- *The Insider* — P.V. Narasimha Rao (political insider fiction)
- *Train to Pakistan* — Khushwant Singh (political moment captured in fiction)
- *Midnight's Children* — Salman Rushdie (nation-as-protagonist)
- *All the King's Men* — Robert Penn Warren (Western comp, political-moral)
- *The Ministry of Utmost Happiness* — Arundhati Roy (recent political Indian fiction)

Pick the right 3–4 for your specific tone.

## Template 11 — Payment Plumbing

| Platform | For | Setup time |
|---|---|---|
| **Substack** | Recurring international + Indian (USD/INR via Stripe) | 1 hour |
| **Buy Me a Coffee** | One-time international (USD) | 30 min |
| **Razorpay payment page** | Native INR — no FX, low friction for Indian donors | 2 hours (requires PAN, bank) |
| **Direct UPI / bank** | Tier-1 inner-circle ₹40K asks. Just send your UPI on a phone call. | 0 min |

**Critical:** Indian donors will pay 4–8% more if you make them use a USD platform. Have INR-native rails ready before you ask Indian sponsors.
