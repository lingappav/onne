## 1. The Honest Math

$100/day is the *average*. Real fundraising is **lumpy** — a few big asks, a steady drip of small ones. Plan for the lumps, not the line.

### Three parallel streams

| Stream | Type | Target Share | Average $/day | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Patrons** (Substack, Buy Me a Coffee, Patreon) | Recurring small | **$3,000** | ~$33/day | Steady drip; compounds |
| **B. Sponsors** (individual mid-tier asks, ₹5K–₹40K each) | One-time mid | **$3,000** | ~$33/day | Lumpy; 20–30 people |
| **C. Big tickets** (1–2 grants, 1 advance / option / corporate) | Large one-off | **$3,000** | ~$33/day | 1–3 lumps in 90 days |

If any one stream lags, the others can carry it. If two lag, the goal is at risk. Do not depend on a single channel.

### Sub-targets

- **By Day 30:** $1,500 raised (16.6%) — early-believer money + first patrons
- **By Day 60:** $4,500 raised (50%) — public launch + first grant decision in
- **By Day 90:** $9,000 raised (100%) — final push, in-person events, top-ups

## 3. The Three Streams in Detail

### Stream A — Patrons ($3,000 target, steady drip)

**Platforms:** Substack (primary), Buy Me a Coffee (secondary), Patreon (optional).

**Math:**
- 50 paid Substack subscribers @ $7/month × 3 months = $1,050
- 30 Buy Me a Coffee one-offs @ $10–$25 average = $500
- 10 "founding members" @ $100–$250 = $1,500

**= ~$3,000**

**Cadence:** publish 1 sample chapter / research piece per week on Substack. Pin a clean ask: *"This work continues because of paid subscribers."*

**Tier design:**
- **Reader** $7/mo — weekly chapter + research notes
- **Insider** $20/mo — above + monthly video call + early excerpts
- **Founding Patron** $100 (one-time) — name in acknowledgments + signed copy
- **Patron-of-Record** $250 (one-time) — above + dedication of a chapter

### Stream B — Sponsors ($3,000 target, individual asks)

Personal-network outreach. Bangalore tech professionals, alumni networks, theatre/film friends, SWA contacts.

**Math:**
- 5 sponsors @ ₹40,000 (~$480) = ~$2,400
- 10 sponsors @ ₹10,000 (~$120) = ~$1,200
- 20 sponsors @ ₹5,000 (~$60) = ~$1,200

Pick **any combination** that lands $3,000. Higher-ticket sponsors are far more efficient — 5 friends at ₹40K beats 50 strangers at ₹4K.

**Asks must be specific:**
- "Will you sponsor one chapter? ₹10,000 covers the editorial pass on it, and your name goes in the acknowledgments under that chapter."
- "Will you sponsor the research trip to Delhi? ₹40,000. I'll send you the field notes."

### Stream C — Big Tickets ($3,000 target, 1–3 wins)

Grants, advances, sponsorships. Lower hit rate, higher payoff.

**Targets:** (full list in [contacts.md](contacts.md))
- **India Foundation for the Arts (IFA)** — Arts Practice grant, up to ₹5L
- **Sangam House residency** — not cash but in-kind; converts to $1,000+ of equivalent writing time
- **Toto Funds the Arts** — small grants for emerging Indian writers
- **PEN/Faulkner Writer's Fund** — emergency grants for working writers
- **Publisher advance** — Juggernaut, Westland, Aleph, Penguin India — $1,500–$3,000 advance possible
- **Existing film-option leverage** — Sunday Cinemas option from Feb 2026 is a *signal of commercial credibility*; use it in every grant application

---

## 6. The Five Hardest Truths to Internalise

1. **Most asks will fail.** A 20% conversion is good. Don't read silence as personal — read it as a vote to ask 5 more people.
2. **Specific asks beat general asks.** "Will you sponsor a chapter for ₹10,000?" outperforms "Please support my book."
3. **Friends and family fund the first $2,000.** Strangers fund the last $4,000. The middle $3,000 is the hard middle. Plan for it.
4. **Show the work publicly throughout.** The Substack publishing rhythm IS the fundraise. Nobody funds an invisible writer.
5. **The film-option is your credibility weapon.** Mention Sunday Cinemas' 24-month option in every cold ask. It is a market validator that 95% of unpublished writers don't have.

---

## 7. Failure Modes & Recoveries

| If by Day 30 you have raised less than $1,000 | Pause outreach. Re-do the one-pager. Test it on 3 trusted readers. Re-launch Day 35. |
| If Stream A has fewer than 10 paid subscribers by Day 30 | The tier pricing or value prop is off. Reduce minimum tier to $5/mo. Add a free chapter giveaway. |
| If Stream C produces no grant by Day 60 | Stream C is unreliable; redouble Streams A + B with revised asks for $4,500 instead of $3,000 each. |
| If you're at $5,500 on Day 75 (behind) | Switch the final push from broad outreach to focused **5-person big asks** — friends who can write ₹40K cheques. |
| If you're at $8,000 on Day 89 (close but short) | Announce the event live-ask is for the last $1,000. Public scoreboards close fast. |
