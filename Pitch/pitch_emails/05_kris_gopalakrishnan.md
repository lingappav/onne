    political crimes done without technology — Partition,
    the Emergency, Bhopal, the 1984 and 2002 riots, the
    long pendency of justice. The argument is that the
    most consequential political crimes were committed
    with paper, persuasion, and bureaucratic compliance,
    not with technology. The novel locates the crime in
    the *chain of consenters*, not in the executioner.

philosopher and a 20th-century European theorist of
totalitarianism — is the intellectual wager of the book.

asking, who consented? Who signed? Who knew and stayed
silent?
