Subject: Grant inquiry — independent novel on Indian
       institutional accountability (film option attached)

To the Programmes Team,
Azim Premji Foundation,

I write to inquire whether the Foundation has a grant or
fellowship route that could support an independent
literary project that sits within the Foundation's broader
concerns about institutional integrity and democratic
accountability in India.

I am Shambhulingappa (Vishwa), a Bangalore-based novelist
and SWA member (No. 46108). My debut novel,
*The President's Rule*, dramatises a constitutional crisis
in Karnataka and the systemic conditions that make such
crises possible. Sunday Cinemas optioned the film rights
in February 2026 for a 24-month period.

The novel rests on six research strands. Two of them sit
directly within the questions the Foundation has
historically supported:

  • One Nation, One Election — the Kovind Committee Report
    (18,626 pages, 18 constitutional amendments
    recommended), the 129th Amendment Bill (2024), and
    the federalism question that lies beneath the
    efficiency case. The novel is interested in what
    happens to a state's civic education when its
    electoral cycle is no longer its own.

  • The greatest political crimes done without technology —
    the Emergency, Partition, Bhopal, the 1984 and 2002
    riots, manual scavenging, judicial pendency. The
    novel locates harm not in the executioner but in
    the bureaucratic chain of consent. Hannah Arendt's
    'banality of evil' is the theoretical frame; six
    Indian case studies are the substrate. The argument
    is that institutional integrity is not a process
    question — it is the only meaningful protection a
    citizen has against state harm.

The novel does not write essays. It dramatises these
questions through character. But the research substrate is
serious and is publicly published, post by post, through a
weekly Substack across the 90-day writing year.

The full project budget is $9,000 over 90 days. I would
welcome an opportunity to discuss whether any line item
could be supported by APF — most appropriately the
research and editorial component (~$2,000) for the two
chapters that engage the questions above.

I attach:
  • One-page pitch
  • Five-page synopsis
  • Chapter 1 (sample, 4,200 words)
  • Note on the six research strands
  • Detailed budget

I welcome the opportunity to discuss the project at any
time convenient to the Foundation team.
