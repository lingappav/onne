I am Shambhulingappa, writing as Vishwa — a Bangalore-based
novelist and SWA member (No. 46108). My debut novel,
*The President's Rule*, dramatises a constitutional crisis
in Karnataka and the social mind that responds to it.
Sunday Cinemas optioned the film rights in February 2026
for a 24-month period.

Two of the novel's six research strands sit at the heart of
the work Infosys Foundation has long supported:

  • Kautilya's Dandaneeti as the philosophical spine of
    the book. The novel treats every act of state power
    — the police shooting, the Governor's signature, the
    media's verdict — implicitly against the Kautilyan
    measure of *tikshna danda* (excess), *mridu danda*
    (laxness), and *yathaarha danda* (just punishment).
    I have already drafted an 11-part scholarly manual on
    this for my research archive; the novel translates it
    into character.

  • Karnataka as the third lead of the novel. The book is
    set across six Karnataka districts — Bengaluru,
    Mysuru, Belagavi, Udupi/Mangaluru, Hampi, and
    Vijayapura. The Vachana tradition (Basavanna, Akka
    Mahadevi, Allama Prabhu), the Vijayanagara
    inheritance, the Mysuru civic compact, and modern
    Bangalore's pluralism are all *present* in the prose,
    not as footnotes but as ambient grammar. Kannada
    appears without italics; the state is treated as a
    character, not a setting.

The full project is a 90-day independent writing fundraise
of $9,000. I am writing to request that Infosys Foundation
consider supporting one of the following defined
components:

  • $2,000 — the Karnataka field-research block (three
    district visits, interviews, archival access at
    Karnataka State Archives, Hampi University, and
    Kannada Sahitya Parishat)
       OR
  • $1,500 — the editorial pass on the Dandaneeti and
    Karnataka chapters, plus the production of a clean
    print-ready limited edition for Karnataka library
    placement (50 copies to Sahitya Parishat, district
    libraries, and selected universities)

I attach:
  • One-page pitch
  • Five-page synopsis
  • Chapter 1 (sample, 4,200 words)
  • The Dandaneeti research manual (excerpt)
  • Karnataka research brief

I would welcome the chance to present the work in person at
the Foundation's office, at any time convenient to your team.

With deep respect for the cultural work Infosys Foundation
has done across Karnataka for three decades,
