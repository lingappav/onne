## Formal Grant Letter

```
Subject: Grant inquiry — independent novel on Indian
       governance and constitutional politics (film
       option attached)

To the Programmes Team,
Tata Trusts,

I write to inquire whether the Trusts have a grant or
fellowship route that could support an independent
literary project of public-interest substance.

PROJECT
*The President's Rule* — a literary political novel by
Shambhulingappa (Vishwa), SWA Member No. 46108. Sunday
Cinemas optioned the film rights in February 2026 for
a 24-month period. The novel dramatises a constitutional
crisis in Karnataka and engages a series of substantive
research questions in Indian governance and political
philosophy.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

  • A literary political novel of approximately 90,000
    words, in first draft, currently in revision.
  • Set in Karnataka, 2024–2026, across six districts.
  • Research substrate of six strands, including
    primary engagement with Kautilya's Arthashastra,
    the Kovind Committee Report on simultaneous
    elections, the constitutional architecture of
    federalism in India, and the moral philosophy of
    state coercion.

ALIGNMENT WITH TATA TRUSTS' PUBLIC-INTEREST WORK

  • Kautilya's Dandaneeti — the original Indian science
    of proportionate state coercion — provides the
    novel's philosophical spine. I have drafted an
    11-part scholarly manual mapping the Arthashastra's
    Books 1, 3, and 4 to modern Indian governance.
    The manual is the substrate; the novel is the
    translation into character. The work positions
    Indian political theory as a living analytical
    tradition, not a museum piece.

  • One Nation, One Election — the Kovind Committee
    Report (18,626 pages, 18 recommended constitutional
    amendments), the 129th Amendment Bill (2024), and
    the federalism question underneath the efficiency
    argument. The novel engages the question seriously
    from multiple positions, drawing on primary
    documents and the published Indian National
    Congress and constitutional-scholar critiques.

PUBLIC-GOOD OUTPUTS

  • Free Substack publication of the underlying
    research, post-by-post, across the 90-day writing
    year — visible, dated, citable.
  • Open-source publication of the Dandaneeti and
    Indrajaala research manuals (already drafted and
    in the project repository).
  • 50-copy limited-edition print placement to
    Karnataka libraries, universities, and the Sahitya
    Parishat.
  • A public Bangalore-launch event on Day 88 of the
    writing year.

REQUESTED SUPPORT
$2,000 (~₹1.7 L) toward the editorial-and-research
component for the Dandaneeti and ONOE chapters, plus
production of the limited-edition Karnataka library
placement.

ATTACHED
  • Detailed proposal (4 pages)
  • Five-page synopsis
  • Chapter 1 (sample, 4,200 words)
  • Dandaneeti research manual (excerpt)
  • Detailed budget with line-item justification
  • Timeline
  • Author CV + SWA membership verification
  • Two references

I welcome the opportunity to discuss the project at the
Trusts' convenience.

Sincerely,
Shambhulingappa (Vishwa)
SWA Member No. 46108
+91 [number]
[substack URL]
```
