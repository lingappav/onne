## Usage Notes

- Each email is **a starting point, not a sent draft.** Fill in `[BRACKETS]` with anything specific to your most recent interaction or knowledge of the recipient.
- **Never send any of these without one personal opening line** that proves you have read or attended something of theirs in the last 90 days. That single line is the difference between cold and warm.
- Most of these go to a **gatekeeper first** (CSO, foundation manager, EA). The email tone reflects that — formal enough to forward up the chain, personal enough that the principal would not be embarrassed by it.
- **The Sunday Cinemas film option from Feb 2026** is the single most powerful credibility line. Keep it in paragraph 2 of every email.
- **All asks are tied to specific deliverables**, not generic "support the book." Patrons fund deliverables, not aspirations.
