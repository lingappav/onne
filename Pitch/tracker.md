## Pipeline (the active list)

Maintain a single living list of named prospects in the pipeline. Status moves left to right.

| Name | Tier | Channel | First touch | Last touch | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name] | T1 / T2 / T3 / Grant / Publisher | call / email / DM / form | YYYY-MM-DD | YYYY-MM-DD | cold → reached → replied → meeting → committed → paid → declined | One line |

**Rule:** never let a prospect sit at "reached" or "replied" for more than 7 days without a follow-up. Either follow up, or move them to "declined" and free your mental space.

### Pipeline-stage thresholds

- **< 50 names in pipeline by Day 14** → not enough top-of-funnel; add 25 names this week
- **< 10 "replied" by Day 30** → the messages are not landing; rewrite the template
- **< 5 "committed" by Day 30** → the ask amount may be too high for the tier; revisit

## Conversion Math (the brutal column)

If by Day 30 you have:
- 30 asks sent and 0 yeses → **your one-pager or your ask amount is wrong.** Rewrite. Don't send more before fixing.
- 30 asks sent and 3 yeses (10%) → **on pace.** Send more.
- 30 asks sent and 9 yeses (30%) → **scale up.** The pitch works. Add 25 names to the list.

Healthy fundraising conversion rates:
- Cold strangers: **2–5%**
- Warm but distant: **10–20%**
- Friends & family: **40–60%** (lower than people think — money asks are awkward)
- Repeat / referral: **50–80%**

If you're below the floor of the relevant band, the problem is the ask, not the people.

## Five-Minute Pre-Send Checklist (before every outreach batch)

- [ ] Recipient's name spelled correctly
- [ ] First sentence is specific to them
- [ ] One clear ask, one clear amount
- [ ] One clear next step (link or "I'll send the link if you say yes")
- [ ] Subject line earns the open
- [ ] If high-value: have I run this past one trusted reader?
- [ ] Tracker updated *before* sending, not after

---

## When to Pause and Re-Strategise

Stop the outreach machine and rethink if:

- Day 21: under $700 raised
- Day 45: under $2,500 raised
- Day 60: under $4,500 raised
- Day 75: under $6,500 raised

A 24-hour pause to fix the funnel is worth ten more bad asks.

---

## When to Celebrate

Celebrate publicly when:
- First $1 in
- 10th patron
- First grant decision (yes OR no — both are progress)
- $3,000 ($1K from each stream — proves the model works)
- 50% mark
- 90% mark
- Close

Each celebration is a public moment that brings in the next wave. Momentum is itself a fundraising tool.
