The President’s Rule is a literary political thriller set in Karnataka in 2026, when a third coalition collapse triggers President’s Rule and Delhi shocks the nation by appointing a Jharkhand oncologist, Dr. Sampath Kumar, as Special Administrator.

He enters a fevered state like a surgeon in an emergency ward: a Rs. 36,000-crore shadow economy, a Rs. 1,400-crore land fraud, a cyberattack on farmers’ welfare payments, a Rs. 500-crore bribe, and finally the most elegant corruption he has ever seen — a 47-page Kodagu pitch deck designed to reclassify 30,000 acres of protected coffee land.

Sampath refuses every comfortable exit. He does not come to transform the system. He comes to prove that ordinary integrity, applied with extraordinary discipline, can briefly bend the machinery of governance toward the public good. The patient is not cured. But the patient is alive — and in India, that is the beginning of everything.
