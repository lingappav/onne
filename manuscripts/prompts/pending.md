# Sunday Cinemas · The President's Rule — Project Roadmap & Vision

## The Core Intention

Complete the novel in 6 months. Not a treatment. Not a pitch deck. The full novel — 25 chapters, 72,000 words, every scene, every character, every political confrontation rendered with the precision of an oncologist's chart. Only then does it move.

The story has a clear beginning, a developed middle, and a strong ending that says exactly what it needs to say. We do not dilute that intention by discussing it with too many people too early. Focus first. Community second.

**Ramenahalli Jagannatha** — producer of two Kannada feature films, director of *Hondisi Bareyiri*, *Teerataroopa Tandeyavarge*, and *Tamma Sukhagamana Bayasuva* — has adopted *The President's Rule* and **will direct it himself.**

This is not a speculative option. This is a commitment.

He is personally mentoring Vishwa Shambhulingappa through the entire writing process — guiding the research framework, shaping the novel's factual grounding in real democratic events, and ensuring the writer has the autonomous space and structured support to complete the manuscript without compromise.

Once the novel is complete, Ramenahalli will bring in experienced screenplay writers to adapt it into film language — not to rewrite it, but to translate its precision into a screenplay that can scale to a commercial box-office result. The novel is the master document. The screenplay serves it.

## Why Write the Novel First — The Full Argument

Half-baked writing causes more damage than no writing at all. A partial script or incomplete treatment invites interference — from producers, from stars, from committees — before the story has the internal strength to resist it. The writer loses their voice. The story loses its spine.

The novel-first model solves this:

1. **A finished novel is defensible.** It has a beginning, middle, and end. It has character psychology on the page. It has tone, world, and argument. Nobody can ask you to "make the hero more likeable" when the novel has already told you exactly who he is and why.

2. **A finished novel commands better terms.** Any producer approaching a complete, credible manuscript with 1,000 pre-committed readers is approaching a finished work — not a hope. The writer negotiates from strength.

3. **Research-heavy content requires depth, not speed.** *The President's Rule* touches President's Rule, constitutional democracy, shadow economies, media manipulation, and the mechanics of Indian bureaucratic corruption. These are not topics you can sketch. They require the kind of research that only a novel — not a screenplay — has the space to contain.

4. **One director. One vision.** Because Ramenahalli has committed to direct, there is no "will a director like this?" uncertainty. The screenplay adaptation will be done with him in the room, shaped by his cinematic sensibility, and executed by writers he trusts. The pipeline is clean.

## The 7-Phase Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — 1,000 Founding Readers (Months 1–2)**
Recruit 1,000 people from the author's immediate circle who will invest ₹1,000 each. Target: youth of India who understand what is happening to democracy and want to be part of a story that speaks to it honestly. This ₹10 Lakh funds the website infrastructure, community platform, and manuscript completion.

**Phase 2 — Manuscript Completion (Months 3–4)**
Vishwa completes all 25 chapters under Ramenahalli's mentorship. Research team operates autonomously. Priority revisions: integrate the "old court case" flashback (Act I), add two Priyadarshini scenes, dramatise the fabricated audio clip (Ch 10), tighten Act II pacing. Craft target: Award-Contender tier (82+/100).

**Phase 3 — Literary Award Circuit (Month 5)**
Submit to JCB Prize for Literature, Crossword Book Award, Sahitya Akademi (English), Vodafone Crossword. Award longlisting transforms the commercial conversation — OTT rights values double on a longlisted manuscript.

**Phase 4 — Publisher Pitches (Month 6)**
Approach HarperCollins India, Penguin Random House India, Westland with: complete manuscript + 1,000 pre-committed readers + Ramenahalli's directorial commitment + award submissions in progress. This is not a cold pitch. This is a term sheet.

**Phase 5 — Screenplay Adaptation (Q3 2026)**
Ramenahalli leads the screenplay team. Experienced writers adapt the novel into film language — scene structure, dialogue compression, visual storytelling. The novel's political authenticity is non-negotiable. The screenplay's job is to make it commercially scalable.

**Phase 6 — Production & Cast (2026–2027)**
Cast announcement. Co-production structure. With a complete novel, a committed director, a finished screenplay, and a publisher attached — this is the strongest possible position from which to approach a star. The star comes to the project. The project does not chase the star.

**Phase 7 — Pan-India Release (2027)**
Theatrical + OTT dual window. Political dramas with authentic scripts (*Gangubai Kathiawadi* ₹209Cr, *The Kerala Story* ₹290Cr, *Sarkar* ₹250Cr+, *Raazi* ₹190Cr) validate the commercial ceiling. Target: ₹1,000Cr with right cast and distribution. The story is already there. Everything else is execution.

## What the 1,000 Founding Readers Get

Access to the creative process — not a press release, the actual process:

- First-edition signed copy of the novel (numbered 1–1,000)
- Chapter-by-chapter manuscript access as it is written
- AI storyboard: 6 key scenes visually directed and updated as the manuscript evolves
- Participation in the climax conversation — the series of incidents are documented, the resolution is still being shaped, founding readers are part of that conversation
- Behind-the-scenes: from Sunday Cinemas' novel-first methodology to the screenplay adaptation process to production decisions
- Name in the film credits and published novel
- Direct connection to a director who has already committed — not a maybe, a decision

## How the ₹10 Lakhs Is Spent

1. **Website and community platform** — kept active with full information about Sunday Cinemas, the novel-first approach, and the writing process. This is the proof-of-concept for every writer who comes after this one.

2. **Research infrastructure** — the novel is grounded in documented real-world political events. The research team needs support to verify facts, secure permissions where needed, and build the factual base that gives the story its credibility.

3. **Writer autonomy** — Vishwa writes with full creative freedom. The ₹10L removes financial pressure from the writing process. No compromise under economic stress.

4. **Community conversations** — structured discussions about the novel's themes, the incidents it depicts, and the democratic questions it raises. The founding 1,000 are not passive — they are an active sounding board whose engagement validates the story's relevance.
