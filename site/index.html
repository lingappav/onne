The Story

A new Chief Minister of Karnataka takes office with a radical idea: what if a politician chose constitutional integrity over power? Dr Sampath Kumar has 90 days to answer two questions: Can a functioning democracy be restored from within? And at what cost?

The President's Rule is a 72,000-word political thriller set in Bangalore, Delhi, and Kodagu. It explores institutional power, democratic collapse, and the price of principles through the eyes of a leader who refuses to play the old game.

About the Book

The President's Rule explores what happens when institutional power meets constitutional integrity. Set across three months in a state facing institutional collapse, the novel follows a Chief Minister who must choose: bend the system to save it, or defend the system and lose everything.
